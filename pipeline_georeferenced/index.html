Georeferenced DSM Pipeline

GeoTIFF → terrain DEM (Copernicus GLO-30) + Depth Anything detail → absolute-elevation DSM → viewable terrain
Choose how to supply the area of interest. An already-cropped GeoTIFF is used as-is. A larger scene is cropped server-side to the bounds you give. With bounds only, the server fetches the least-cloudy Sentinel-2 scene itself (Earth Search STAC), which is the slowest option. Bounds are EPSG:4326 degrees, at most 0.5° per side.
