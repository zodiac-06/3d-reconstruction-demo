Georeferenced DSM Pipeline

GeoTIFF → depth → SRTM-calibrated absolute-elevation DSM → viewable terrain
Scope requirement: the uploaded GeoTIFF must already be cropped to your area of interest. This pipeline does not search for scenes or crop them for you — that's a separate, earlier step (see qgis_prep/01_fetch_geotiff.py + 02_crop_geotiff.py, or crop by hand in QGIS). Uploading an uncropped, multi-hundred-MB scene will still run, but slowly, and will fetch an SRTM tile sized to match whatever area you gave it — not a sensibly small one.
