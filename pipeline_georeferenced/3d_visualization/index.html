Navigation

Controls

Orbit: Left-drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.
Fly: WASD to move, drag mouse to look, Q/E to roll.
Probe: click the terrain for lat/lon, elevation and slope.
Measure: press Measure, then click two points.
